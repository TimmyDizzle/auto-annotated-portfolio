# Platform Copy — Titles, Captions, Hashtags, Hooks

## Hook variants (on-screen text, 0:00–0:03)

A/B these across platforms — same video, different opening text:

1. "POV: you're a small creator and progress is measured in subscribers"
2. "Nobody celebrates like a small creator"
3. "I throw a party every time I hit a subscriber milestone. It's getting out of hand."
4. "Billy Madison but it's my subscriber count"
5. "My party budget has officially exceeded my ad revenue"

Variant 3 is the safest opener (sets premise + promises escalation).
Variant 4 works only where the reference lands — better for YouTube than IG.

## Pinned comment (both platforms)

> Official milestone party schedule:
> 100 ✅ 500 ✅ 1,000 ✅ 2,500 ✅ 5,000 ✅
> 10,000 = I rent a bouncy castle. This is a threat.

The pinned comment sets up the series (see series-plan.md) and gives
commenters something to riff on — "what should the 10k party be" is
free engagement.

## Reply bait

Seed these as reply prompts if comments are slow:
- "What should the 10k party be? Wrong answers only."
- "The $1.37 is real by the way"
- "The kazoo guy is available for bookings"

## Posting notes

- Post the Short and the Reel natively on each platform — don't cross-post
  with watermarks (both algorithms downrank them).
- Best windows for comedy content: weekday lunch (11am–1pm) and evening
  (7–9pm) in your audience's timezone. Check your own analytics after the
  first week and re-anchor.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour, in character where possible
  (grateful, slightly delusional milestone-party guy).
