# Props, Wardrobe & Budget

The joke is that the parties are absurd, not expensive. Homemade-looking
props are funnier than good ones. Total spend target: **under $100** —
which itself becomes a caption joke ("party budget: 73x the ad revenue").

## Wardrobe (escalates every milestone)

| Milestone | Outfit | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Grind shots | Hoodie, worst one you own | Closet |
| 100 subs | Graduation cap + gown | Amazon (~$20) or borrow |
| 500 subs | Same hoodie + party hat | Dollar store |
| 1,000 subs | Suit (fit doesn't matter, worse = better) | Closet / thrift |
| 2,500 subs | Sash + plastic crown | Dollar store (~$8) |
| 5,000 subs | Blazer over t-shirt (press-conference core) | Closet |

## Deliberately cheap > accidentally cheap

Every prop should look like the creator made it themselves the night
before, because in-universe, they did. If a prop looks professionally
made, rough it up.
