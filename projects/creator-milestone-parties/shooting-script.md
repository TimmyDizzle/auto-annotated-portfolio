# Shooting Script — "Milestone Parties" (Anchor Video)

**Runtime:** 55–60 seconds · **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical · **Tone:** dead sincere

Audio legend:
- 🎹 = sad lo-fi piano (grind shots)
- 🎉 = DING notification → celebration horn + confetti blast (identical sound
  every milestone — repetition is the rhythm of the joke)

---

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:03

**INT. BEDROOM "STUDIO" — 3AM.** Dark room, lit only by monitor glow.
CREATOR, hoodie, hunched over desk, eating instant ramen. Refreshes the
analytics page. Nothing. Refreshes again.

- 🎹 audio
- **On-screen text:** "POV: you're a small creator and progress is measured
  in subscribers"

---

## MILESTONE 1: 100 SUBS — 0:03–0:10

🎉 DING. **SMASH CUT:**

Same room, lights on. Creator in a full **graduation cap and gown**. A
printer-paper banner taped to the wall: **"100!!"** One sad balloon. He fires
a confetti popper, then delivers a tearful Oscars acceptance speech — to
his cat.

> CREATOR *(voice cracking)*: "They said one hundred people would never
> care. Well… ninety-four of them aren't even my relatives."

Toasts with sparkling grape juice in a plastic champagne flute.

- **On-screen text:** "MILESTONE: 100 SUBSCRIBERS"

---

## MILESTONE 2: 500 SUBS — 0:10–0:18

🎹 (2-second grind shot: same desk, refreshing) → 🎉 DING. **SMASH CUT:**

A **piñata shaped like a play button** hangs from the ceiling fan. Hired
entertainment: **one guy with a kazoo**, playing his heart out. Creator
lights **indoor sparklers**. The smoke alarm goes off mid-celebration. He
keeps dancing. A neighbor bangs on the wall.

> CREATOR *(shouting over the alarm)*: "THE EMPIRE GROWS!"

- **On-screen text:** "500 SUBSCRIBERS. THE EMPIRE GROWS"

---

## MILESTONE 3: 1,000 SUBS — 0:18–0:27

🎹 grind beat → 🎉 DING. **SMASH CUT:**

**INT. RENTED BANQUET HALL.** Full wedding-reception energy. Three guests.
A DJ leans into the mic:

> DJ: "MAKE SOME NOISE FOR… MONETIZATION ELIGIBILITY!"

Creator, now in a suit, cuts a ceremonial ribbon and unveils a **cardboard
silver play button** (the real one requires 100k). Behind him, a champagne
tower built entirely from **instant ramen cups**.

- **On-screen text:** "1,000 SUBS — LEGALLY ALLOWED TO EARN MONEY"

---

## MILESTONE 4: 2,500 SUBS — 0:27–0:36

🎹 grind beat → 🎉 DING. **SMASH CUT:**

**EXT. DRIVEWAY — DAY.** A ticker-tape parade for no one. Creator, wearing
a **sash and plastic crown**, rides through the sunroof of his mom's Corolla
doing a beauty-queen wave to zero spectators. A drone flies overhead towing
a tiny banner: **"2.5K"**. A folding table displays commemorative t-shirts
nobody bought.

> CREATOR *(waving, regal)*: "Thank you. Thank you all."
> *(there is no one)*

- **On-screen text:** "2,500 SUBSCRIBERS. A DYNASTY."

---

## MILESTONE 5: 5,000 SUBS — 0:36–0:48

🎹 grind beat → 🎉 DING. **SMASH CUT:**

Super Bowl treatment: fireworks overlay, confetti storm. **HARD CUT** to a
"press conference" — folding table, single microphone, one reporter (it's
the kazoo guy, now holding a notepad).

> CREATOR *(podium blazer, solemn)*: "First, I'd like to thank the
> algorithm."

He proudly holds up his first AdSense check. **SLOW ZOOM** on the amount:

## **$1.37**

**FREEZE FRAME.** Hold the beat. This is the thesis shot — longest beat in
the video.

- **On-screen text:** "5,000 SUBSCRIBERS 🏆" → "First paycheck: $1.37"

---

## PUNCHLINE — 0:48–0:55

**HARD CUT** back to the dark room. 3AM. Same ramen. 🎹

He refreshes the page. His face falls.

- **On-screen text:** "4,999. Someone unsubscribed."

---

## CTA — 0:55–0:60

Creator turns, dead-eyed, straight down the lens. Three-second deadpan hold.

- **On-screen text:** "Subscribe. The next party is on you."

**END.**

---

## Performance notes

- Every reaction to the DING is pure, unironic joy. Every grind shot is
  pure, unironic despair. No middle gears.
- The DING smash cuts must be framed identically (same dark-room setup) so
  the repetition lands as a rhythm.
- Escalate wardrobe every milestone: hoodie → grad gown → suit → sash and
  crown → podium blazer. Costume escalation reads instantly on a phone.
