# Shot List — Shooting Order

Grouped by setup so the whole video shoots in one day plus one exterior
afternoon. Phone camera is fine — over-produced kills the joke.

## Setup A: Dark room "grind" shots (shoot all together, ~30 min)

Same framing every time. Lock the phone on a tripod, kill the lights,
monitor glow only.

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| A1 | Cold open: hunched at desk, ramen, refresh, refresh | 5–6s of usable footage |
| A2 | Grind beat before 500 | 2s, identical framing |
| A3 | Grind beat before 1,000 | 2s |
| A4 | Grind beat before 2,500 | 2s |
| A5 | Grind beat before 5,000 | 2s |
| A6 | Punchline: refresh → face falls ("4,999") | Hold the reaction 3s |
| A7 | CTA: slow turn to lens, dead-eyed 3s stare | Shoot 3 takes, pick the deadest |

## Setup B: Apartment party — 100 subs (~45 min)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| B1 | Wide: grad gown, banner, balloon, confetti popper fires | Popper is the cut-in point |
| B2 | Medium: tearful speech to the cat | Cat's indifference is the joke — get a cutaway of the cat looking away |
| B3 | Close: grape juice toast in plastic flute | Label visible = funnier |

## Setup C: Apartment party — 500 subs (~45 min)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| C1 | Wide: piñata on ceiling fan, kazoo guy mid-performance | Kazoo guy plays it dead serious |
| C2 | Sparklers light → smoke alarm audio → keep dancing | Alarm can be added in post |
| C3 | Cutaway: fist banging on wall (neighbor) | Shoot your own hand on the other side of the wall |

## Setup D: Banquet hall — 1,000 subs (~1 hr, or over-decorate the apartment)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| D1 | DJ announcement into mic | "MONETIZATION ELIGIBILITY" needs a real DJ-voice read |
| D2 | Ribbon cutting + cardboard silver play button unveil | Cardboard must look homemade |
| D3 | Ramen-cup champagne tower reveal | Build it 5 cups tall minimum |
| D4 | Wide showing exactly 3 guests clapping | Empty chairs in frame = funnier |

## Setup E: Driveway parade — 2,500 subs (exterior, ~1 hr)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| E1 | Car rolls slowly, creator through sunroof waving | 5 mph max, sash + crown |
| E2 | Ticker tape: someone off-frame throws shredded paper | Two handfuls, loop it in post if needed |
| E3 | Drone with tiny "2.5K" banner overhead | Or fake it: banner on a fishing pole from a ladder |
| E4 | Cutaway: t-shirt table, zero customers | Static 2s shot |

## Setup F: Press conference — 5,000 subs (~45 min)

| # | Shot | Notes |
|---|------|-------|
| F1 | Podium blazer, folding table, single mic: "thank the algorithm" | Play it like a championship presser |
| F2 | Kazoo guy as reporter, raises hand, takes notes | Same actor = running gag pays off |
| F3 | **THE CHECK.** Holds it up proud → slow zoom to $1.37 → freeze | Shoot 5 takes. Slowest zoom wins. Protect this shot |
| F4 | Fireworks/confetti storm | Overlay in post (CapCut effects) — don't buy fireworks |

## Post checklist (CapCut is plenty)

- [ ] Identical DING sound + horn on all 5 milestone cuts
- [ ] Sad lo-fi piano bed under all Setup A shots
- [ ] Confetti overlay on milestone 5
- [ ] Slow zoom + freeze frame on the check
- [ ] Burned-in captions on every line (85% watch muted)
- [ ] Export 9:16, keep under 60s for Shorts eligibility
