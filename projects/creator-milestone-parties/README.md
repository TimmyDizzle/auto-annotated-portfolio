# Creator Milestone Parties — Production Kit

A satirical Instagram Reel / YouTube Short about the starving-artist digital
creator, where every subscriber milestone triggers a wildly disproportionate
celebration (Billy Madison style). Maximum party, minimum payoff.

## The comedic engine

Two contrast dials, both turned up at every milestone:

1. **The party gets bigger** — props, guests, production value escalate.
2. **The achievement stays objectively tiny** — 100 subs is nothing; even
   5,000 pays $1.37.

Play everything 100% sincere. Nobody winks at the camera. The creator
believes every party is deserved. That's the Sandler rule.

## Format

- 9:16 vertical, 45–60 seconds (anchor video)
- Cold open (3s) → 5 escalating party vignettes (~7–8s each) → punchline (5s)
- Audio: sad lo-fi piano on grind shots, hard cut to celebration horn +
  confetti sound on every DING. Same two sounds every time — the whiplash
  is the joke.

## The one thing to protect in the edit

The $1.37 AdSense check reveal. It's the thesis of the whole satire —
maximum celebration, minimum payoff. Give it the slowest zoom and the
longest beat in the video.

## Rights note

Keep the Billy Madison *structure*, not the movie's clips or audio.
Original staging keeps the video clear of copyright strikes on both
platforms.
