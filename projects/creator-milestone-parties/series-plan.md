# Series Plan — The Milestone Party Franchise

The anchor video is a one-off joke. The franchise is the growth engine.

## The mechanic

The CTA — **"Subscribe. The next party is on you."** — turns the joke into
a participation loop:

1. Viewers subscribe to *fuel* the bit ("help this idiot hit 1,000").
2. Every real milestone triggers a real party video: guaranteed content.
3. Escalation pressure ("what's he going to do at 10k??") becomes a
   channel-wide retention hook.

The satire becomes documentary. That's funnier, and it compounds.

## Release plan

| # | Video | Trigger | Notes |
|---|-------|---------|-------|
| 0 | Anchor video (the 60s escalation) | Post now | Establishes the premise and the sound/cut grammar |
| 1 | "We hit [next real milestone]. You know what that means." | Real milestone | Full party video, 20–40s, same DING/horn sounds |
| 2+ | One party per real milestone | Each real milestone | Escalate one notch each time; take party ideas from comments |
| — | Compilation re-cut | Every 3–4 real parties | Re-post the anchor + real parties as a new compilation — the video re-posts itself |

## Party escalation ladder (for real milestones)

Commit to these publicly (pinned comment) so each one is a promise:

- **10,000** — bouncy castle in the driveway
- **25,000** — ice sculpture of the subscriber count
- **50,000** — hire a wedding band for a 30-second set
- **100,000** — the *real* silver play button arrives; hold a "retirement
  ceremony" for the cardboard one (full military-honors tone)

The cardboard play button retirement is the long-game payoff — it only
works because video 0 established the cardboard one. Plant flags early,
pay them off months later.

## Running gags to maintain

| Gag | Rule |
|-----|------|
| The DING → horn smash cut | Identical sound and framing, every video, forever |
| Kazoo guy | Appears at every party in a different role (entertainment, press, security, officiant) |
| The cat | Always present, never impressed |
| The check | Update the amount at each real milestone — track revenue vs. party budget on screen |
| Party budget vs. ad revenue | Caption every party with both numbers. The gap closing over time IS the channel's story arc |

## Why this works as marketing, not just comedy

- **Format recognition**: same sounds + same cut = instantly recognizable
  in-feed. Format familiarity is what makes series bingeable.
- **Built-in shareability**: "help him hit the next milestone" gives
  viewers a reason to send it to friends — the share is participation.
- **Milestones are the content calendar**: growth generates the videos,
  so the channel never runs out of episodes of its best-performing bit.
